## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: No Third-Party Analytics
The site SHALL not include third-party analytics trackers.

#### Scenario: Visitor loads any page
- **WHEN** a user loads a public page
- **THEN** no third-party analytics tracker is loaded

### Requirement: No Analytics Consent Banner
The site SHALL not display a cookie consent banner for analytics tracking.

#### Scenario: Visitor opens the site
- **WHEN** a user visits the site
- **THEN** no analytics consent banner is shown
